Japanese is one of the most rewarding languages in the world to learn — and one of the most misunderstood. It isn't just memorising kanji or mastering honorifics. It's discovering an entirely different way of seeing the world.

This is a personal corner of the internet where I share what I know: grammar guides, reading practice on topics I love, and honest lessons from someone who has been teaching for years.

Whether you're just beginning with hiragana, or preparing for the JLPT N1, pull up a chair. There's a page here with your name on it.
